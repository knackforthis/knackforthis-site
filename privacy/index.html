Privacy Policy

Short version: we collect minimal analytics to understand traffic and improve the site. We don’t sell personal data.

Last updated:

What We Collect

KnackForThis uses Google Analytics (via Google tag / gtag.js) to measure general site usage (pages visited, approximate location, device/browser type, and referral sources).

  • Analytics data: aggregated usage signals to understand what’s working.
  • Emails you send us: if you contact us, we receive whatever you include in your message.

What We Don’t Collect

  • No accounts and no requirement to submit personal details to browse the site.
  • No intentional collection of sensitive personal data.
  • No selling of personal data.

Cookies and Tracking

Google Analytics may use cookies or similar technologies to help measure site performance. You can limit tracking by using browser controls, blocking third-party scripts, or using privacy tools/extensions.

Third-Party Services

Our site may link to third-party websites. Their privacy practices are theirs, not ours.

Data Requests

If you want to request deletion of an email you sent us, or you have a privacy concern, contact: fitdepth@gmail.com

Note: Because this site doesn’t require logins, we typically don’t have a reliable way to identify an individual visitor in analytics data. Most analytics is aggregated.
