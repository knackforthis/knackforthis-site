Carpentry

Carpentry is not one thing. It ranges from fast, rough structural work to slow, highly visible precision. This page explains the reality of the craft, then lets you test fit — first broadly, then by specialization.

Take the Carpentry Diagnostic

What Carpentry Actually Demands

Across all types, carpentry rewards people who tolerate repetition, measurement, material constraints, and mistakes that cost time. The difference between subtypes is where the pressure comes from: speed, visibility, clients, or precision.
